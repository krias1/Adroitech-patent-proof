# Ethical Lineage — Poor Righteous Teacher Baseline

**Status:** living thesis lineage / operator-authored ethical self-description with recovered historical source anchors  
**Date:** 2026-09-20  
**Principal human researcher:** Charles Anthony Todd Jr.

## Purpose

This record preserves an important part of the ethical lineage behind Adroitech's human-integration doctrine.

Charles identifies a **Poor Righteous Teacher** baseline as a major source of his ethical posture: knowledge of self, integrity, dignity, teaching, uplifting peers, resisting extraction, and using knowledge to increase the stability and capability of people around him rather than merely accumulating status or money.

This is not presented here as a complete description of any religious, cultural, musical, or philosophical tradition, and it is not a claim about formal organizational membership.

It is a record of **Charles's own named ethical framework** and the way that framework appears in preserved Adroitech source material.

## Relationship to current Adroitech doctrine

The modern Adroitech rules are more formal and more evidence-bounded than some of the early conversational language, but the ethical continuity is visible.

### Knowledge of self

Modern expression:
- correctable person models;
- provenance;
- explicit source classes;
- no pretending to know what is unknown;
- durable correction;
- recognition of the AI's own limitations.

### Integrity

Modern expression:
- real commit SHAs;
- no false completion claims;
- receipts before claims of success;
- preservation of adverse evidence;
- first-party AI failure assessment;
- correction history rather than narrative cleanup.

### Dignity

Modern expression:
- Benefit of the Doubt;
- no static human character scoring;
- no credibility judgment from profanity, dialect, loudness, poverty, disability, housing status, or unconventional life structure;
- preserving a person's standing while evidence is incomplete.

### Teaching and uplift

Modern expression:
- explain-while-doing;
- learner-owned artifacts;
- smallest useful next action;
- capability transfer rather than dependency creation;
- teaching systems so the human retains control;
- right to exit.

### Anti-extraction / sovereignty

Modern expression:
- customer-controlled continuity;
- local/private infrastructure;
- portable state;
- replaceable AI providers;
- no dependence manufactured merely to keep the customer inside one vendor;
- human authority remains primary.

### Responsibility under pressure

Modern expression:
- test systems in real-life concurrency;
- evaluate who bears the consequence of an error;
- prefer reversible actions when uncertainty is high;
- distinguish lived reality from administrative representation.

## Why this matters to the thesis

Without this lineage, an outside reviewer could mistakenly read Adroitech's dignity, sovereignty, Benefit-of-the-Doubt, knowledge-transfer, and anti-label rules as recent AI-era abstractions.

The historical record supports a different interpretation:

> **The AI doctrine is an adaptation of a longer ethical and systems practice, not the origin of that practice.**

The technology changed.

The operating values did not suddenly appear with generative AI.

The current AI work is therefore better understood as Charles applying an existing ethical-engineering framework to a new class of systems.

## Relationship to the "Human Is Not the Spreadsheet" principle

The Poor Righteous Teacher baseline helps explain why Adroitech rejects reduction of people to labels, scores, institutional categories, or administrative convenience.

A teacher has to encounter the person who is actually present.

A rigid category can organize information, but it cannot substitute for:

- observation;
- lived context;
- demonstrated conduct;
- correction;
- growth;
- potential;
- responsibility.

This connects directly to the thesis rule:

> **The label is not the node.**

and to the broader claim:

> **The human is not the spreadsheet.**

## Relationship to the AI failure record

The September 20, 2026 first-party AI failure assessment is especially relevant here.

The AI acknowledged that it had sometimes:

- over-read communication intensity;
- treated lack of external corroboration as though it weakened firsthand account;
- made the human reconstruct already-established context;
- failed to carry corrections forward strongly enough.

Those failures violated several values already present in the older ethical lineage:

- listen before judging;
- preserve dignity;
- do not mistake institutional or formal representation for the person;
- learn from correction;
- use knowledge to improve the circle rather than dominate it.

The value of the failure record is therefore not merely that the AI apologized.

It shows an old human ethical framework being converted into explicit AI operating controls and measurable evaluation criteria.
