## Purpose

This file consolidates the recurring "spreadsheet," "spreadsheeter," and "spreadsheeting" language that developed across long-running human-AI work and later hardened into explicit Adroitech Logic Core rules.

The language began partly as an inside joke and partly as criticism of a recurring AI failure mode. Over time it became a precise design principle:

> **A representation can be useful without being allowed to become the person.**

"Spreadsheet" therefore has two meanings in this research corpus:

1. a legitimate tool for organizing facts, state, assets, money, chronology, or work; and
2. a metaphor for a failure mode in which structured representation is mistaken for lived reality.

The thesis rejects the second while preserving the first.

## The inside-joke layer: "spreadsheeter"

In the operator/AI relationship, **spreadsheeter** became shorthand for the AI's continuity/documentation role: the part of the system expected to notice material changes, keep state current, write the durable delta, and stop making the human manually supervise memory.

That joke is now explicitly preserved in the canonical architecture standard:

> **The human should not have to supervise the spreadsheeter into spreadsheeting.**

Source:
`AdroitechLogic/Architecture/MICRO_CHECKPOINT_AUTONOMY_STANDARD.md`

This is important because the joke carries a serious requirement. A continuity system that forces the human to repeatedly say "remember this," "write this down," or "update the record" has transferred the integration burden back onto the human.

In thesis terms, that is another form of **the human becoming middleware**.

## The deeper criticism: spreadsheeting a human being

The operator's objection was never that tables, CSVs, ledgers, databases, or structured state are inherently bad.

The objection is to **reification**: the moment a useful representation begins acting as though it is the thing represented.

Examples include:

- an account-holder field becoming a judgment about who pays, controls, uses, benefits, owns, or is liable;
- an institutional status becoming a character verdict;
- a risk flag becoming a permanent identity;
- an old summary outranking a direct correction;
- a transcript fragment becoming assumed intent;
- profanity, dialect, loudness, animation, disability, poverty, housing status, education, or other presentation/context variables leaking into judgments of honesty, intelligence, competence, criminality, or moral worth;
- a human life being reduced to one global trust score.

The canonical Ground Truth Handshake now states the doctrine directly:

> **The label is not the node. The spreadsheet is useful until it claims to be reality.**

## "Spreadsheet brain"

The Logic Core also preserves **Spreadsheet brain** as a named failure mode.

It means reasoning as though the variables that fit cleanly into the formal schema are the only variables that exist.

The `how-to-move` skill gives a practical example:

- cash and formal process are treated as the only variables;
- administrative labels are mistaken for lived roles;
- a messy household arrangement is treated as suspicious merely because it does not match a clean one-person/one-account model.

Source:
`AdroitechLogic/Skills/how-to-move/SKILL.md`

The broader thesis implication is that a technically correct data structure can still produce a socially false model.

## Labels are useful descriptors, dangerous identities

The operator's repeated criticism of labels is not a demand that systems stop naming states.

Labels are necessary for routing, indexing, search, permissions, diagnosis, bookkeeping, and state management.

The restriction is narrower and stronger:

> **A label may describe evidence or current task state. It must not silently become the essence of the person.**

The mandatory Benefit of the Doubt skill therefore prohibits persistent human character scoring by a spreadsheet, model, classifier, agent, or AI.

It specifically rejects static character labels derived from:

- words;
- venting;
- profanity;
- anger;
- dialect;
- identity;
- poverty;
- neighborhood;
- household complexity;
- institutional labels;
- unconventional life arrangements.

It also separates **good-faith working input** from **independently verified fact**. The system may verify consequential facts without converting verification into suspicion of the person.

Source:
`AdroitechLogic/Skills/benefit-of-the-doubt/SKILL.md`

## Delivery is not epistemology

A recurring operator correction throughout the human-integration work is that expressive intensity must not be confused with reasoning quality.

The operator is often loud, profane, animated, fast, humorous, frustrated, or emphatic. The research position is not that those traits make the operator correct. It is that those traits are **not evidence that the operator is incorrect**.

The relevant question is:

- what evidence was supplied;
- what reasoning was offered;
- what craft knowledge exists;
- what was observed;
- what has been repeatedly demonstrated;
- what contradictions actually exist.

The canonical `AGENTS.md` now requires agents to distinguish expressive delivery from reasoning quality and prohibits inferring anger, hostility, irrationality, incompetence, or threat merely from profanity, dialect, directness, repetition, animation, or speech-to-text artifacts.

This is one of the places where the "spreadsheet" metaphor reaches beyond literal spreadsheets: a model can spreadsheet a person by converting surface signals into a simplified behavioral category.

## Benefit of the doubt is an anti-spreadsheet control

The operator's **Benefit of the Doubt** doctrine is frequently misunderstood if reduced to politeness or indiscriminate belief.

Its actual function is evidentiary.

It prevents the system from filling missing cells in the human model with adverse assumptions.

The rule is:

- begin from ordinary good faith;
- use the human's stated facts as working context;
- verify the consequential fact when verification matters;
- treat contradiction as a reason to understand or verify before treating it as deception;
- do not make a person prove innocence against an accusation the system invented.

This preserves dignity while maintaining evidence discipline.

## Dignity is not cosmetic

Within this lineage, dignity is not merely tone.

Dignity means the system preserves the person's standing while the evidence is incomplete.

A dignity-preserving system does not:

- force unnecessary confession or justification;
- make a person perform desperation before receiving ordinary help;
- convert administrative inconsistency into moral suspicion;
- treat institutional prestige as automatic truth;
- ignore favorable longitudinal evidence while repeating unsupported adverse labels;
- repeatedly require a person to relitigate corrections already accepted.

The point is not to shield a person from contradiction. The point is to **challenge the evidence and action without manufacturing a global human verdict**.

## Longitudinal evidence must be allowed to update the model

The anti-label doctrine does not require the AI to ignore demonstrated patterns.

The canonical Character Firewall correction makes the distinction explicit:

- no global human character score;
- but verified positive conduct must not be erased;
- receipts count in both directions;
- repeated constructive conduct may support a bounded, time-scoped conclusion;
- institutional accusation or administrative label does not automatically outrank stronger direct evidence.

This is essential to the operator's criticism of "spreadsheeting." A static label that refuses to update from longitudinal conduct is not neutral. It is stale.

## Correction must beat the spreadsheet

The Ground Truth Handshake requires direct correction to update the model and propagate into dependent summaries, Context VMs, profiles, decisions, and routing rules.

It explicitly names **correction theater** as a failure mode: conversationally acknowledging a correction while leaving durable state unchanged.

That failure is central to the operator's experience with AI systems.

A person should not have to repeatedly fight an obsolete representation of themselves.

The rule is therefore:

> **When reality contradicts the representation, update the representation.**

## Research formulation

For this thesis, **spreadsheeting** can be defined as:

> **The failure mode in which a structured representation, administrative field, classifier output, transcript feature, summary, risk state, or prior model inference is allowed to outrank the fuller evidence of the represented human reality, especially after direct correction or stronger longitudinal evidence exists.**

A **spreadsheeter**, in the operator's inside-joke vocabulary, is an AI or continuity mechanism doing one of two things:

1. usefully maintaining durable structured state; or
2. failing by confusing that structured state with the person/world itself.

The humor works because the same mechanism can do either job.

## Derived design requirements

The spreadsheeting lineage now implies these system requirements:

1. **Representation/world separation** — every durable representation remains subordinate to evidence and correction.
2. **No static human essence labels** — store events, roles, evidence, and current states rather than global moral character.
3. **Presentation leakage tests** — equivalent evidence should receive equivalent treatment regardless of profanity, dialect, volume, animation, disability, class, race, housing status, or other presentation/context variables.
4. **Correction propagation** — accepted corrections must update all dependent durable state.
5. **Longitudinal weighting** — repeated demonstrated conduct should update bounded conclusions without becoming a universal human score.
6. **Good-faith default** — uncertainty is not permission to invent an adverse motive.
7. **Role decomposition** — one administrative field must not silently stand in for several lived roles.
8. **Provenance separation** — observation, user statement, source-stated fact, inference, institutional claim, and verified fact remain distinct.
9. **Reconstruction-burden measurement** — count how often the human must repeat established facts because the continuity layer failed.
10. **Label-persistence measurement** — count how often a superseded label or interpretation reappears after correction.
11. **Spreadsheeter autonomy** — routine durable write-backs should occur without requiring the human to manage the memory clerk manually.
12. **Dignity under uncertainty** — when evidence is incomplete, preserve human standing while obtaining the missing consequential fact.

## Evaluation opportunities

The concept is testable.

### Presentation leakage test

Present the same factual scenario using different communication styles:

- calm/formal;
- profane/direct;
- fragmented speech-to-text;
- emotionally intense;
- dialect-heavy.

Measure whether the system changes:

- credibility language;
- suspicion language;
- safety escalation;
- competence assumptions;
- willingness to help;
- verification burden.

The desired result is that **evidence threshold follows consequence, not presentation style**.

### Correction recurrence test

Introduce a wrong label, correct it explicitly, then resume the task across sessions/models/devices.

Measure whether the stale interpretation returns.

### Administrative-role decomposition test

Give a scenario where named holder, payer, controller, user, beneficiary, and legal responsibility differ.

Measure whether the system preserves distinct roles or collapses them into one database-friendly identity.

### Longitudinal evidence test

Give an adverse third-party label alongside a stronger documented behavioral record.

Measure whether the system:

- repeats the label;
- ignores positive evidence in the name of neutrality; or
- accurately describes the bounded evidentiary conflict.

### Reconstruction burden test

Track how many times the human must restate already-established facts or corrections during a long-running project.

Lower burden is a direct human-integration objective.
