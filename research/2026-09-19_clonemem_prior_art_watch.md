## Why preserved

CloneMem evaluates long-term memory for highly personalized AI using longitudinal non-conversational digital traces, including diaries, social-media posts, and emails spanning one to three years. It evaluates tracking of evolving personal states and reports that current memory mechanisms struggle with life-grounded personalization.

## Differentiation pressure

This is relevant prior-art / research-pressure evidence against broad claims that merely using long-lived personal traces, emails, longitudinal histories, or evolving user-state tracking is distinctive by itself.

Adroitech differentiation should therefore remain at the integrated runtime/governance layer where supported by evidence: permissioned person state, provenance and freshness, correction/supersession, context/workstream isolation, bounded action authority, receipts, portable continuity, model/provider substitution, and customer sovereignty/right-to-exit.
