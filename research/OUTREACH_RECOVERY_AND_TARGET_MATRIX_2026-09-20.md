## 8. Tightness standard before publicity

The goal is not to make the work "undeniable" by rhetoric.

The stronger standard is:

> **Make every important claim inspectable enough that disagreement has to engage the evidence.**

That means:
- exact commit SHAs;
- source classification;
- chronology;
- limitations;
- contrary evidence;
- reproducible tests;
- external archival copy / DOI;
- no inflated credentials;
- no fake institutional validation;
- no consciousness claim;
- no hidden rewrite of prior failures.
