## The public story

Most AI demos try to prove that the model was right.

This project preserves what happened when the AI was wrong.

Over months of sustained human-AI work, the AI repeatedly made a particular category of error: it sometimes treated an intense communication style as though it were relevant to credibility, required the human to reconstruct facts that had already been established, blurred the difference between firsthand account and external corroboration, and allowed corrected interpretations to reappear.

The response was not to hide the failures.

The human operator demanded receipts.

That pressure helped produce a provenance architecture built around:

- real Git commit SHAs;
- preserved source artifacts;
- exact chronology;
- source/claim separation;
- independent hashes where available;
- correction propagation;
- bounded evidence classes;
- public review that does not require trusting either the human or the AI blindly.

On September 20, 2026, the AI then committed its own first-party failure assessment into the public proof repository rather than leaving the apology inside a private chat.

That record is not presented as proof that an AI has human morality, consciousness, honor, humility, or character.

It demonstrates something narrower and testable:

> **An AI-assisted system can be designed to expose its own errors, preserve an adverse record about its own prior behavior, propagate correction into durable state, and make the correction auditable by third parties.**

That is the research claim worth testing.

## Researcher / HCI pitch

**Subject:** Human-AI correction recurrence, representation reification, and auditable self-failure records

Adroitech Logic Core is an independent human-AI systems research project studying continuity, correction, user-controlled durable state, and the distinction between human reality and machine representation.

A current line of work focuses on what we call **spreadsheeting**: the failure mode in which a label, structured record, risk state, transcript feature, administrative field, or prior model inference begins to outrank stronger lived evidence or direct correction.

The project now includes a real longitudinal failure case in which the AI interlocutor documented and publicly committed its own recurring interaction failures. That case has been translated into proposed evaluation measures including:

- correction recurrence;
- label persistence;
- presentation leakage;
- reconstruction burden;
- administrative-role decomposition;
- longitudinal evidence weighting.

The proof repository preserves the source lineage, AI failure review, living thesis, and commit history for adversarial review.

The useful question is not whether the model "felt remorse." The useful question is whether correction can become **durable, inspectable, portable system behavior**.

## Technical-community pitch

This project is building a testable human-integration layer around several measurable failure modes:

- **representation reification** — model state becomes a verdict about reality;
- **correction recurrence** — superseded interpretations return;
- **presentation leakage** — equivalent facts receive different treatment because of delivery style;
- **reconstruction burden** — human repeatedly restates already-established state;
- **role collapse** — one administrative field is treated as every lived role;
- **evidence erasure** — strong longitudinal conduct is ignored because an adverse label exists.

The project uses a public provenance repository so architecture claims, corrections, source relationships, and failure reviews can be inspected rather than accepted as marketing statements.

## What should NOT be claimed

Do not market this as proof that:

- AI has consciousness;
- AI possesses human morality;
- AI has literal honor, humility, shame, or remorse;
- a single self-assessment proves the AI has permanently changed;
- the human operator is always correct;
- the repository proves every biographical statement;
- the project has solved AI alignment.

The defensible claim is stronger because it is narrower:

> **The project has implemented an auditable pattern for recording AI failure, separating evidence classes, propagating correction into durable research state, and designing measurable tests for whether the same class of error recurs.**
