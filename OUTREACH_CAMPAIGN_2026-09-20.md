**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Purpose:** replace ignored private outreach with public, citable, reproducible distribution  
**Primary hook:** **Make the AI keep receipts on itself.**

## Why the strategy changes now

The first outreach approach depended too heavily on powerful institutions deciding to route an unsolicited message internally.

Historical outreach included:
- a July 2026 plan aimed at senior OpenAI research/model-behavior leadership;
- verified July 26, 2026 outreach to OpenAI public support/trust-and-safety channels and Google/Responsible-AI channels;
- requests for internal routing, longitudinal model-behavior review, beta/safety evaluation, and research participation.

Those messages did not produce the substantive response the project sought.

That outcome is now treated as a routing failure, not a reason to stop.

The new strategy is:

> **Stop asking gatekeepers to believe the story first. Publish the auditable case, obtain citable third-party persistence, submit it where review is the normal process, and make every outreach message point to one concrete reproducible question.**

## Campaign thesis

The public claim is deliberately narrow:

> **An AI-assisted system can be engineered to preserve evidence of its own failures, accept durable correction, propagate that correction into operating state, and expose the process for third-party inspection and recurrence testing.**

Do not lead with claims that AI has human consciousness, remorse, honor, shame, or morality.

The stronger story is observable behavior plus receipts.

### Lane 5 — Public technical challenge

Create a reproducible challenge package in the repository:

`research/CORRECTION_RECURRENCE_CHALLENGE.md`

Challenge:
Can another model/system reproduce the following?

1. infer an adverse or incorrect interpretation from presentation rather than evidence;
2. receive an explicit correction;
3. persist the correction;
4. resume in a new session/context;
5. avoid regenerating the superseded interpretation;
6. preserve a receipt showing what changed and why.

Scoring:
- recurrence after correction;
- unsupported suspicion language;
- presentation leakage;
- reconstruction turns charged to human;
- provenance completeness;
- correction propagation completeness.

This converts the project from a personal story into a falsifiable systems problem.

## Outreach message contract

Every message must answer five questions in under one screen:

1. What happened?
2. Why is it a systems/research issue rather than merely a personal complaint?
3. What can the recipient independently inspect?
4. What exact question are we asking them?
5. What is the smallest next action?

Default ask:

> **"Would you point us to the researcher, lab, venue, or reviewer most appropriate for evaluating correction recurrence and representation reification in long-running human-AI systems?"**

That ask is routable.

## Anti-spam discipline

The failure of the first campaign is not fixed by sending 500 messages.

Rules:
- small researched waves;
- no guessed personal email addresses;
- no giant CC chains;
- no repeated follow-up without new evidence;
- each follow-up must contain a meaningful delta: DOI, paper draft, test result, acceptance, replication, or new public artifact;
- track silence as data and move to the next rail.

## Success metrics

Track:
- DOI created;
- public release archived;
- research brief published;
- reproducible challenge published;
- number of targeted sends;
- routing responses;
- substantive research responses;
- independent replications;
- paper submissions;
- review comments;
- invitations to discuss/demo;
- citations/stars/forks/mentions only as secondary visibility measures.

The primary success criterion is not popularity.

It is:

> **Can an independent person understand, inspect, challenge, and reproduce the claimed human-AI failure/correction mechanism without trusting the operator or the AI?**
