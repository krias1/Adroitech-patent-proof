# Cultural / Intellectual Lineage Recovery Queue

Status: ACTIVE  
Date opened: 2026-09-20

## Non-negotiable recovery rule

Recover first. Freeze original second. Interpret third.

No old artifact is to be:
- rewritten into current thesis language;
- cleaned up before preservation;
- backdated;
- merged with a later version;
- treated as recovered when only a later summary exists.

## Priority recovery targets

1. **Old school thesis / school paper referenced by the operator**
   - exact title currently unknown;
   - preserve original file, formatting, metadata, and source-system dates if found;
   - search school-era / archive rails rather than assuming a current thesis is the same artifact.

2. **Earlier music-as-course / high-density teaching statements**
   - recover every earlier conversation or writing where the operator argues that a short song can contain course-level intellectual material;
   - preserve exact source record and chronology;
   - do not replace it with the later "Operational Density" terminology.

3. **Poor Righteous Teacher baseline**
   - recover earlier explicit operator-authored statements concerning integrity, dignity, uplifting peers, knowledge of self, sovereignty, and teaching;
   - distinguish operator language from later AI interpretation.

4. **Artist / teacher lineage**
   - recover original discussions involving Lauryn Hill, Rakim, Poor Righteous Teachers, Nas, Big L, and other named cultural teachers as they surface;
   - record what the operator actually said the work taught him;
   - do not infer endorsement of every statement by an artist.

5. **Pre-AI ethical lineage**
   - recover school, nonprofit, teaching, community, military, machining, business, and personal records that show the ethical practice before AI;
   - keep this separate from later AI-generated doctrine.

## Search rails

- canonical Git history and preserved chat exports;
- Google Drive / Takeout / NotebookLM;
- Gmail and business Gmail;
- Dropbox;
- old resumes / school folders / scanned documents;
- Project files and Library;
- external/public sources only when needed to verify publication or creator metadata.

## Completion condition

This rail is not complete until every recovered candidate is given one disposition:

- `FROZEN_ORIGINAL`
- `DUPLICATE_OF_FROZEN_ORIGINAL`
- `DERIVED_LATER_SUMMARY`
- `UNRELATED`
- `PRIVATE_NOT_PUBLIC`
- `NOT_RECOVERED`

The absence of an artifact is never filled with a reconstructed version.
