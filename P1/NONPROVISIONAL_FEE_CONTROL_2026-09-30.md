# Nonprovisional Fee-Complete Control — 2026-09-30

## Purpose

This control converts the generic fee TODO in `NONPROVISIONAL_READINESS.md` into a current USPTO baseline for the operative 20-claim / 3-independent-claim utility application. It does **not** assert an entity status that has not been affirmatively established.

## Current USPTO baseline checked 2026-09-30

Authoritative source: USPTO fee schedule and Nonprovisional (Utility) Patent Application Filing Guide.

Baseline totals before any other applicable fee:

- Regular: **$2,000**.

- Small entity, if the USPTO electronic-filing basic-fee line applies in Patent Center: **$730**.

The operative candidate set is 20 total claims and 3 independent claims. On that count alone, no excess-claim fee is presumed here; this must be rechecked against the exact final claims and the live Patent Center fee calculation immediately before certification.

## Fees/surcharges that must be affirmatively checked before READY

1. **Entity status.** Do not pay a discounted fee until entitlement to small or micro entity status has been affirmatively established under the current rules. Micro entity requires the applicable certification.
2. **DOCX surcharge.** Description/specification, claims, and abstract should be filed in DOCX unless a deliberate justified exception is made. The current USPTO filing guide states that the additional 37 CFR 1.16(u) fee applies when those application parts are not filed in DOCX.
3. **Non-electronic filing fee.** Avoid by filing the utility application electronically through Patent Center.
4. **Excess claims.** Recalculate if the final set exceeds the fee-free claim thresholds or contains multiple dependent claims that change the fee calculation.
5. **Application-size fee.** Calculate from the final application under the live USPTO rules after exact-file QA.
6. **Late surcharge.** Do not intentionally defer filing/search/examination fees or the oath/declaration. The USPTO filing guide states that paying required filing/search/examination fees later than the filing date, or filing the required oath/declaration later, triggers a late surcharge.
7. **Other specialized fees.** Sequence listings, large tables, computer program listings, petitions, or other specialized submissions remain N/A only after affirmative review.

## Filing rule

The final zero-reconstruction packet must contain the live Patent Center fee calculation, entity-status basis/certification if discounted status is used, exact claim count, independent-claim count, application-size determination, DOCX determination, and payment receipt after submission.

No `READY` status may be assigned from the baseline numbers in this document alone. USPTO notes that fees normally change in October; therefore the live fee schedule and Patent Center calculation must be rechecked on the actual filing date.
