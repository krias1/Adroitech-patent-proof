Current USPTO ADS guidance states that, for applications filed on or after September 16, 2012, when a juristic entity such as a corporation or association is identified as an applicant, the ADS must be signed by a patent practitioner. The same guidance recommends generating the Web-based ADS in Patent Center for a new utility application and warns that benefit claims can fail electronic recognition if ordered incorrectly.
