## Disposition

**PASS_EVIDENCE / FINAL-SPEC-AND-CLAIM QA PENDING**

This control addresses only the utility requirement of 35 U.S.C. §101. It does not establish eligibility under Alice/Mayo, novelty, nonobviousness, enablement, definiteness, inventorship, or allowance.

Current USPTO MPEP §2107 requires at least one credible assertion of specific and substantial utility for each claimed invention; utility may also be well-established where a person of ordinary skill would immediately appreciate why the invention is useful from its characteristics. Utility evidence must maintain a nexus to the claimed subject matter as disclosed.

## Independent-family utility mapping

### Claim 1 — integrated runtime resolution
Specific practical utility: operating a computer-implemented inference/runtime system from a bounded current-world slice selected from durable state and writing material resulting changes back to authoritative durable state, permitting later runtime operation from updated durable state rather than requiring the full prior transcript as the operative continuity substrate.

Substantiality: this is an immediate operational use of the claimed process in computing systems, not a research objective requiring later discovery of a use.

Credibility: the claimed operations are ordinary-computing-capable data selection, runtime use, and persistent state update mechanisms. Nothing in the asserted utility depends on an extraordinary scientific proposition. Frozen-P1 support remains controlled separately in the claim-support records.

Dependent Claims 2-8 narrow that same operational utility through provenance/evidence/correction/freshness/permission filtering, inference-resource budgeting, lifecycle relevance, correction/supersession, model-independent reconstruction, physical-identifier resolution/write-back, and offline capture/reconciliation. Each retains the practical utility of Claim 1 while applying a concrete narrower operating constraint or continuity mechanism.

### Claim 9 — workstream continuity under Person State change
Specific practical utility: resuming a persistent computer-managed workstream using stored workstream/checkpoint information while incorporating mutable Person State and newer evidence, so the resumed execution context can reflect changed current conditions without discarding the durable workstream.

Substantiality: the process has an immediate use in persistent/resumable computing workflows; it is not merely an invitation to conduct further research.

Credibility: persistence, checkpoint/resume data, state updates, and construction of a resume package are technically ordinary-capable computing operations. The asserted utility does not require accepting an implausible scientific proposition.

Dependent Claims 10-14 retain and narrow this utility through field-specific Person State/freshness, correction or supersession before resume, persistent do-not-repeat state, mobile-feasibility transition, and bounded resume-package construction.

### Claim 15 — provider-independent handoff
Specific practical utility: reconstructing execution context at an authorized runtime or endpoint from provider-independent durable state so continuity can survive a change of inference provider, runtime, or endpoint without making one provider's transcript or endpoint-local state the sole authority.

Substantiality: this is an immediate computing-system continuity and portability use, not a speculative future research use.

Credibility: serialization/persistence, authorized retrieval, reconstruction, runtime transfer, permission filtering, and durable write-back are technically credible computing operations. The utility assertion does not contradict contemporary technical knowledge.

Dependent Claims 16-20 retain and narrow this utility through provider/local-cloud transfer, correction/provenance/permission/evidence/do-not-repeat state, permission-scoped reduction, authorized replacement-endpoint reconstruction, and post-reconstruction durable write-back.

## Adversarial limits

1. This utility control does not import an unclaimed result into any claim. The final specification and final claims must maintain a nexus between each asserted practical utility and what is actually claimed and disclosed.

3. Utility PASS does not cure an enablement defect. §112(a) enablement remains a separate filing-critical gate.
4. This control does not rely on philosophy, social benefit, commercial success, novelty, or nonobviousness as substitutes for utility.
5. Final filing specification should state practical technical uses consistently with the frozen-P1-supported mechanisms and must not introduce later matter as though entitled to P1 priority.
