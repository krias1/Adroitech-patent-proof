## Entity-status control
Do not select a reduced fee merely because the applicant is an individual or small business. Small-entity entitlement must satisfy 37 CFR 1.27, including rights/obligation-to-assign constraints. Micro-entity treatment requires satisfaction of 37 CFR 1.29 and the required certification before paying at the micro rate. USPTO currently describes small-entity discounts as 60% and micro-entity discounts as 80% for qualifying fees.

Authoritative entity-status source checked 2026-10-04: https://www.uspto.gov/patents/apply/save-on-fees

## Filing-completeness control
The sprint policy is to file the oath/declaration and all known initial fees with the initial package rather than intentionally invite a later surcharge/notice. USPTO guidance confirms that later filing of inventor declarations or applicable filing fees can trigger a surcharge. The initial package should therefore include the executed declaration(s), ADS, and all fees known to be due at certification.

Authoritative source checked 2026-10-04: https://www.uspto.gov/patents/apply/when-patent-applications-are-incomplete-or-missing-information
