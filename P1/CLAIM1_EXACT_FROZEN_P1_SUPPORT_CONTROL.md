## Operative Claim 1 core

Claim 1 recites an integrated runtime-resolution method combining: (1) person-specific namespace/entity/relationship records; (2) Person State separate from durable world state and carrying freshness metadata; (3) independently resumable workstream records/checkpoints; (4) current input; (5) entity/alias resolution inside the namespace; (6) bounded record selection jointly informed by resolved entity/alias, Person State, and selected workstream; (7) current-world-slice construction; (8) AI inference using that slice plus current input; and (9) material durable write-back separate from conversational transcript data.

## Breadth controls

1. Claim 1 is supported as a **combination**. Generic entity resolution, checkpointing, retrieval, AI inference, or durable storage standing alone must not be represented as the inventive distinction.
2. `bounded subset` must remain tied to the disclosed current-world-slice/relevance procedure; this control does not support arbitrary selection unconstrained by the disclosed state classes and gating factors.
3. `Person State` is not a generic user profile. The frozen P1 treats it as current/time-bounded operational circumstance maintained separately from durable world state.
4. `workstream record` is controlled by the disclosed independently resumable Context VM architecture; this control does not establish priority for every possible generic task record.
5. `separately from conversational transcript data` is supported only to the extent the exact P1 discloses durable operational state independent of original conversation history / transcript authority. Do not broaden that phrase into an unsupported prohibition on all transcript-derived evidence.
6. Later implementation receipts and proof atoms may corroborate conception/reduction to practice but cannot repair a missing P1 limitation.

## Adversarial patentability consequence

Exact-P1 written-description support does not establish novelty or nonobviousness. Claim 1 must still survive the centralized material-information register, including the stateful-runtime/checkpoint art and entity-resolution art, individually and in reasoned combinations. The defensible center is the concrete integrated arrangement and its technical state-selection/write-back behavior, not the existence of any one component.
