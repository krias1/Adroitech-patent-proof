## Skill evidence rule

Every personalized or modular skill discovered during the historical sweep gets an entry here and, where supported, links to:

1. earliest preserved conception/source evidence;
2. earliest formal skill artifact;
3. origin commit / source-system timestamp;
4. Git blob SHA and independent SHA-256 where available;
5. predecessor and successor skill records;
6. implementation/runtime use;
7. acceptance test or behavior test;
8. execution/run receipt or field evidence;
9. material write-back or correction behavior;
10. related P1 proof IDs and P1 coordinates.

A skill is not treated as merely a filename. The proof target is the technical behavior and its lifecycle inside the disclosed operating architecture.
