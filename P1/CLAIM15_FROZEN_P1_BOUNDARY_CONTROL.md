# Claim 15 Frozen-P1 Boundary Control

**Claim family:** Claim 15 — Provider-independent handoff (independent), with Claims 16–20 dependent.

**Status:** NOT READY — exact filed-artifact reconciliation required.

## Purpose

This control prevents a dangerous support error during the nonprovisional sprint: treating pre-filing patent-workspace drafts, later repository architecture, implementation receipts, or post-P1 continuity documentation as though they were automatically part of the exact frozen P1 disclosure filed on 2026-09-16.

The operative support matrix currently identifies candidate Claim 15 coordinates as Background; Summary; Detailed Description §§1, 13–14; and FIGS. 1 and 6. Those coordinates are routing leads only until reconciled against the exact frozen P1 specification/drawings.

## What the pre-filing workspace appears to disclose

The pre-filing `PROVISIONAL_PATENT_APPLICATION_DRAFT.md` contains strong candidate language concerning:

- person-specific context stored independently from any one model's internal weights or conversation context;
- customer-controlled durable representations;
- serialization of material state so another authorized AI runtime, model, session, device, or provider can reconstruct person-specific operational state without the original conversation;
- a Continuity Hypervisor coordinating live human state, current AI runtime state, and durable state;
- FIG. 6 described as cross-provider continuity in which a first AI runtime checkpoints a bounded person/workstream representation and a second AI runtime reconstructs state without original conversation history.

These are highly relevant candidate support passages. They are **not themselves proof of what was filed**. The draft expressly identifies itself as a pre-filing provisional specification draft.

## Frozen-P1 gate

Before Claim 15 can receive written-description or priority clearance, the reviewer must inspect the exact preserved P1 filing artifact and record, limitation by limitation:

1. the exact Claim 15 limitation text;
2. exact frozen-P1 page/paragraph/section coordinates supporting that limitation;
3. whether the support is express or necessarily implicit;
4. the corresponding frozen drawing/figure relationship where material;
5. whether the claimed breadth is commensurate with the frozen disclosure;
6. whether any limitation exists only in a draft, later implementation, later explanation, or later-added technical material;
7. whether `without the original conversation`, `transcript-independent`, `provider-independent`, `bounded representation`, and any handoff-content requirements are actually supported at the breadth claimed.

If a limitation cannot be established from the exact frozen P1, it must be narrowed, removed from the P1-benefit claim, or routed as later matter. Later evidence may corroborate conception/implementation but may not backfill P1.

## Dependent-claim propagation

Claims 16–20 inherit Claim 15's unresolved P1 support gate. Their additional limitations require separate exact-frozen-P1 reconciliation; support for the independent claim does not establish support for the dependent additions.

## Adversarial prior-art boundary

Existing material-information controls already show substantial pre-P1 pressure on generic stateful runtime, checkpoint/resume, provider abstraction, security/permission controls, and workstream machinery. Therefore Claim 15 cannot rely on those generic concepts alone. Any proposed distinction based on the structured handoff representation or transcript independence must first survive this frozen-P1 support gate and then separate §102/§103 analysis.
