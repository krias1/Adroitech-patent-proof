# Claim 2 Exact Frozen-P1 Support Control

**Claim:** Claim 2 — provenance/evidence/correction/freshness/permission filtering.  
**Parent:** Claim 1 — integrated runtime resolution.  
**Priority anchor:** U.S. Provisional Application No. 64/155,744, filed/acknowledged 2026-09-16.  
**Boundary:** exact frozen P1 specification/drawings only. Later implementation evidence cannot enlarge this priority boundary.

## Operative added limitation

Claim 2 depends from Claim 1 and further requires that selecting the bounded subset comprises applying at least one of **source provenance, evidence confidence, correction or supersession state, freshness, or permission scope** to candidate records before inclusion in the current world slice.

## Exact-frozen support finding — CONTROLLED

The frozen P1 discloses the recited filtering dimensions as concrete record metadata and selection controls, not merely as aspirational policy:

- P1 §8 attaches source identity/provenance, timestamps, confidence, permission scope, and supersession status to material records and distinguishes directly observed, user-declared, repository-verified, externally sourced, inferred, uncertain, and superseded information.
- P1 §§8–9 describe candidate records being ranked, gated, weighted, or activated using freshness/recency, evidence confidence, correction/supersession status, and permission qualification as selection variables for the bounded/current world slice.
- P1 §8 expressly permits permission filtering before information is placed into the current world slice; restricted subgraphs need not be exposed to every runtime or user.
- The frozen claim-support coordinates identify Summary / DD §§7–8 and FIGS. 3 and 7 for the Claim 2 family. The operative claim uses an `at least one of` alternative, so priority support does not depend on proving that every listed factor is mandatory in every embodiment.

This is consistent with the already-controlled frozen-P1 findings for Claims 17 and 18, which independently confirm the filed provenance/correction/evidence/permission machinery and permission filtering before current-world-slice emission. That later claim-control work is corroborative only; the priority basis remains the frozen P1 itself.

## Breadth fences

1. `source provenance` remains tied to the filed source/source-identity/source-reference machinery; it is not an unlimited generic audit concept.
2. `evidence confidence` is a record/selection attribute used in bounded-context construction; the claim does not require any undisclosed statistical calibration method.
3. `correction or supersession state` covers the filed correction/supersession machinery and must not be broadened into every conceivable version-control mechanism.
4. `freshness` is tied to timestamps, recency, staleness/freshness rules, and field/state relevance disclosed by P1.
5. `permission scope` is a pre-inclusion gate/qualification on candidate records; the claim does not require disclosure of withheld-record existence or counts.
6. This control clears written-description/priority mapping only. It does not clear enablement, §101, §102, §103, §112(b), §112(f), inventorship, best mode, material-information, public-disclosure, drawing-wide QA, or final filing QA.

## Disposition

Claim 2 is **EXACT_FROZEN_P1_SUPPORT_CONTROLLED** for §112(a) written-description mapping and P1 priority, subject to Claim 1's parent limitations and all remaining independent statutory and filing gates.
